WT_N pairs lab

Generates WT_N pairs with WT_N's own generator (copied verbatim from index.html): each sequence is played in full by one participant (the cards completed before the offer are the sunk cost, the winning cards found by then stay found) and truncated at the offer by another (0 cards completed, only the winning cards still missing, the same cards ahead, chance per box and alternative). The offer comes at random before the last winning card (regimes 1 and 2); in regime 3 it is drawn independently and, if the last winning card comes first, never made.

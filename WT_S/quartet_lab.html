WT_S quartet lab

Each design (WT_S's own generator, copied from index.html by tools/sync_wts_code.js) played in four versions: full / truncated × no gift / g keys gifted, the same g in both gift versions. 1a full: x keys made before the offer. 1b full + gift: g keys gifted at the start (Starting keys), then the x made, then the offer (x + g in hand). 2a truncated: no keys, the offer at the start. 2b truncated + gift: g keys gifted, the offer at the start. Every key, made or gifted, comes with its share of extra white cards (one in front of each covered pair): a covered pair costs 1 + 1, as a key and a new card do, so the keys save nothing and every card costs the same to reach from the offer in all four versions: the same decision. g is even: x × U(25%, 75%), at least 2. Checked on each design: the effort expected to the winning card (staying) at the offer and after each orange card found empty, the cost of staying with switching allowed, the best move. The rows are aligned card by card: each slot of the deck in one column pair, its extra white card (if any) on the left. Under each row: the work from the offer to reach each card, and in blue the effort expected at the offer (left) and after each orange card found empty (under that card).
Contrasts: 2b − 2a: keys in hand with no effort (keys as opportunities to waste); 1a − 2a: as now (made keys); 1b − 1a vs 2b − 2a: the same gift with and without the effort of making keys.

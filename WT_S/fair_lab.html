WT_S fair lab

WT_S's own generator (copied from index.html by tools/sync_wts_code.js), nothing forced. The deck is shown as its slots: from the offer on every slot costs 2 cards of work, in all four versions. Each slot is orange with the chance d (the density, flatDeck: (2K/E)·e−2s/E, the last slot 1), drawn one by one with the debt. Measured at the offer and after each orange card found empty, staying:
effort expected: cards of work to the winning card, never switching (the winning card equally likely on the orange cards left);
fair (deck): the alternative A at which staying, with switching allowed later (the best way), costs A, on the orange cards drawn;
fair (density): the same on the mass of the orange cards: slot s holds the winning card, if no earlier one does, with chance ds / Σt≥s dt. It depends on the position only. This is what the alternative is set on (WT_S, 2026-11-05).
The alternative (black) is drawn around the fair value (density) at the offer.

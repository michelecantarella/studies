How it works here

  • The cards (white and orange) are the same deck for both versions. Every card is locked 🔒 and is opened with a key; the keys can be used only from the offer on.
  • A key is one card (brass). Keys are unlimited. Full: the keys are made before the offer (6 here). Truncated: the same 6 keys are given at the start, with the offer.
  • Keys available is the button that uses a key (at the offer it is the stay button); Alternative cards is the switch, as in WT_Q. The winning card is card 13.

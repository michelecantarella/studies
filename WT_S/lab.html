WT_S pairs lab

Generates WT_S pairs with WT_S's own generator (copied from index.html by tools/sync_wts_code.js, the same code the task uses; the decks are WT_Q's): the deck drawn so that the effort expected stays the same at the offer and after each empty orange card (density with debt, as WT_P); the last card always orange; one winning card, equally likely on any orange card. Every card of the main deck is locked and needs a key (white cards too, in order); a key is one card of work in the keys deck. Full: before the offer only keys are made, x of them (2, 4, 6, 8 or 10, at random): the sunk effort. Truncated: a new deck, no keys, the same alternative offered at the start. The x keys look like a discount but are not one: the first x/2 slots of the full deck are two cards each (a buffer white card, then the slot's card), so the keys cover x cards of which x/2 are extra white cards. Unlocking a covered pair costs 2 cards of work (1 + 1), as a key and a new card do in the truncated deck, which is the slots alone. Every orange card costs the same to reach in both versions: the same effort expected at the offer and after each empty orange card, the same alternative, the same best move.
After the offer there is one way of staying: the cards in order, with a key each; when none is left, a key card first (the game does it by itself). Each pair's keys at the offer can be changed (blue box): the buffer white cards move in front of the new covered slots and both versions are recomputed.
The truncated rows are drawn under the full ones, with a gap under each buffer white card: each new card sits under the covered card it stands for. Under each deck: staying = from the offer, the effort expected to the winning card never switching (each key and each card counts 1), on the card that step works (a covered pair, white then orange or white, is one step: one number, on its second card): red when switching is the best move before that step, green otherwise. Hover any cell for the details. Figure: one pair drawn for the paper (keys, main deck, truncated deck, alternative, the work to reach each card), downloadable as SVG.
